WHAT WORKS FOR YOU

Strong Voice

Unconventional Structure

WHAT DOESN'T WORK FOR YOU

    

      

        
Weak Writing

        

        
Flat, serviceable, or forgettable prose — the dominant risk signal

      

      

        
High Concept Risk

        

        
Great premise that fails to deliver on its central idea

      

      

        
Series Fatigue

        

        
Book 4+ in a series where earlier entries got declining reviews

      

      

        
Repetitive

        

        
A sequel that retreads familiar ground without adding something new

      

      

        
Slow Pacing

        

        
Genuinely meandering pacing — not just contemplative or quiet

      

      

        
Inaccessible Prose

        

        
Dense or difficult writing that requires active effort to parse

      

      

        
Vibe Clash

        

        
A strong genre or tone mismatch — cozy, self-help, or pure romance with no dark edge

Sharing your reading history with Inkling


  
There are two easy ways to get started: export your Goodreads library, or create a simple spreadsheet yourself. We recommend at least 100 rated books including a mix of favorites and misses for detailed, accurate predictions.


  
  
From Goodreads

  

    

      1
      
Go to goodreads.com on a browser (not the app)

    

    

      2
      
Click My Books in the left sidebar

    

    

      3
      
Click Import and Export at the bottom of the sidebar

    

    

      4
      
Click Export Library — the CSV downloads automatically

    

  


  
  
Make your own spreadsheet

  

    

      
Title
Book title

      required
    

    

      
Author
First Last or Last, First

      required
    

    

      
My Rating
0–5 (0 = unrated)

      required
    

    

      
Date Read
YYYY/MM/DD

      optional
    

    

      
Reading Status
read · currently-reading · to-read

      optional
    

    

      
ISBN / ISBN13
Helps match books more accurately

      optional
